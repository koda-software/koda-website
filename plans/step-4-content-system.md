# Step 4: Content System

Purpose: define the copy direction and reusable content blocks before implementation. This step keeps the website clear, credible, and aligned with what Opero can truthfully do today.

Approved choices:

- Voice: Hybrid. Business-readable copy with technical proof underneath.
- Homepage balance: Balanced. Koda Soft and Opero appear together; Opero is the backbone of Koda Soft, while the dedicated Opero page will go deeper.
- Proof style: Combined. Use both product capability proof and engineering discipline proof.

## 1. Content Rules

### Voice

Koda Soft should sound like expert builders who can explain complex systems clearly.

Use:

- Clear business language first.
- Technical proof immediately after the promise.
- Confident but restrained claims.
- Concrete verbs: model, adapt, automate, govern, connect, search, audit, assist.
- Human workflow language: teams, handoffs, approvals, records, decisions, operations.

Avoid:

- Empty SaaS language: unlock, supercharge, revolutionize, seamless, all-in-one, game-changing.
- AI hype: autonomous company, AI runs everything, magic, instant decisions.
- Unsupported maturity claims: every industry, every ERP module, instant implementation, no-code everything.
- Fake trust: invented metrics, fake testimonials, fake logo strips.

### Message Order

Every major section should follow this rhythm:

1. Say the business problem in plain language.
2. Explain how Opero or Koda Soft solves it.
3. Add a concrete capability that proves the claim.
4. Keep the next action obvious.

Example rhythm:

> Your operations change faster than rigid software can keep up. Opero gives teams configurable records, workflows, rules, permissions, and AI assistance so the system can evolve with the way work actually happens.

### AI Language

Use AI as practical operational assistance, not spectacle.

Good framing:

- AI where work happens.
- AI inside queries, rules, scripts, and operational workflows.
- AI that helps teams draft, classify, summarize, transform, and decide with context.
- Human-controlled AI assistance with governance around the data and process.

Avoid:

- AI employees.
- AI replaces your team.
- Fully autonomous ERP.
- AI magic box.

### ERP Language

Use `adaptive ERP` as the primary phrase.

Support it with explanations:

- flexible business software
- operational platform
- configurable company data
- workflow automation
- governance and permissions
- AI-assisted operations

Avoid making the website sound like a traditional rigid ERP vendor.

## 4. Proof Points

### Safe Product Proof

Use these publicly:

- Configurable modules, objects, fields, forms, and relationships.
- Rules that react to records, schedules, manual actions, and changes.
- AI-assisted saved queries, script generation, and rule steps.
- Roles, permissions, audit logs, organization isolation, and module controls.
- Global search, advanced lists, saved layouts, dictionaries, and API tokens.
- Contractor management as a concrete current module example.

### Safe Engineering Proof

Use these publicly:

- Flexible architecture for changing operations.
- Maintainable systems built around real workflows.
- Inspectable automation and auditability.
- Performance-conscious, static-first public website.
- Product-grade execution instead of one-off agency delivery.

### Claims To Avoid

Do not claim:

- Opero covers every ERP domain out of the box.
- Opero is fully no-code for everything.
- AI autonomously runs the company.
- Implementation is instant.
- Opero replaces every industry system.
- There are broad integrations not backed by current evidence.
- There are customers, logos, testimonials, or metrics unless real and approved.
