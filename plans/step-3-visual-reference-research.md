# Step 3: Visual Reference Research

Purpose: collect visual references for Koda Soft before narrowing the final look. This version intentionally scraps the previous agency/product-studio board and focuses on software houses, software engineering companies, and high-tech software companies that signal knowledge, expertise, architecture, and serious technical capability.

Research lens:

- Software houses and product engineering companies first.
- Software companies only when their visual language strongly signals high-tech expertise.
- Avoid generic agency polish, lifestyle branding, and typical SaaS landing-page tropes.
- The website should communicate: Koda Soft knows how to build complex software systems.

## Strongest References For Koda Soft

The most relevant mix appears to be:

- SoftwareMill for engineering depth, technology categories, and expert credibility.
- Thoughtworks for knowledge authority and strong technical opinions.
- STX Next for AI/data/cloud proof structure and enterprise trust signals.
- Blackbird for a direct expert-team voice and playbook-style explanations.
- Vercel for high-tech minimal polish and performance confidence.

## Revised Visual Territory

Recommended territory:

> High-tech software house with product-grade engineering expertise.

This should feel less like a digital agency and more like a team that builds operational software, AI-enabled workflows, data systems, platforms, and serious product infrastructure.

## Desired Visual Signals

- Technical authority.
- Systems thinking.
- Engineering precision.
- Knowledge and expertise.
- Product-grade execution.
- AI as an operational capability.
- Calm confidence, not hype.
- Premium but not decorative.

## Design Motifs To Explore

- Architecture diagrams.
- System maps.
- Data-flow lines.
- Modular grids.
- Code/schema-like fragments.
- Product UI fragments used as evidence.
- Technical notes, labels, annotations, and callouts.
- Subtle blueprint / engineering-document references.
- High-quality diagrams explaining how Opero models operations.

## Palette Direction Ideas

Do not decide final colors yet, but explore territories like:

- Graphite / off-white / signal green / warm amber.
- Deep ink / soft gray / electric cyan / muted lime.
- Charcoal / parchment / steel blue / precise orange.
- Light technical editorial base with dark high-contrast technical panels.

Avoid:

- Generic purple SaaS gradients.
- Neon AI spectacle.
- Dark-only cyber aesthetic.
- Template agency black-and-white minimalism without technical substance.

## Typography Direction Ideas

Explore pairings that feel technical and expert:

- Editorial serif or humanist display for authority plus precise mono for technical details.
- Strong grotesk/sans for software-company clarity plus mono annotations.
- Avoid default Inter/Roboto/system-stack blandness unless the final design intentionally uses them with strong supporting identity.

## Koda Soft Implications

The Koda Soft site should make a visitor feel:

- These people understand complex systems.
- This is a real software engineering company, not a design veneer.
- Opero is built by people who care about architecture, data, workflows, governance, and maintainability.
- AI is part of the engineering stack, not a marketing sticker.
- The company can translate messy operations into reliable software.

## What This Means For The Homepage

Likely homepage hero direction:

- Text-led, but supported by a high-tech system visual.
- Visual should show operations becoming structured software: records, workflows, permissions, AI, search, automation.
- Use diagrams and technical annotations instead of stock screenshots or generic SaaS dashboard mockups.
- First viewport should make Koda Soft feel like the expert builder, while Opero remains the flagship proof.

## Anti-References

Avoid visual patterns that say:

- Generic SaaS startup.
- Marketing agency.
- AI wrapper.
- Outsourcing shop.
- Template site.
- Overly corporate IT consultancy.

## Open Questions For Narrowing

- Should the final direction feel more light/editorial or dark/high-tech?
- Should Koda Soft lead with engineering expertise first, or should Opero dominate the first viewport as the product proof?
- How much code/schema/diagram language should be visible to non-technical buyers?
- Do we want the site to feel more European software house, developer-platform, or enterprise systems company?
