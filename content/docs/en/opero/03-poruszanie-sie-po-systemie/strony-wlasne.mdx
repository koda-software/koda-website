## What a custom page is

A list shows records of one kind. A custom page is something else: a **standalone screen** that gathers into one view what is meant to be together, even though it comes from different places in the system. A department dashboard, a start panel for new employees, a screen with summaries for the management.

A page has its own address and its own entry in the menu. You open it the same way as a list, but you will not find on it a single record form to fill in.

## Contents of a custom page

- **Counters**, that is, single values: the number of open matters, the value of offers this month, the number of documents to approve.
- **Tables** with the results of a summary prepared beforehand.
- **Tasks** from processes, with the option to go to the matter.
- **Charts and reports** prepared by the administrator.
- **Information blocks** with an instruction, an announcement, or links.
- **Buttons** that open another screen or start an action.

> **Example.** The sales department dashboard shows a counter of this week's inquiries, a table of offers awaiting a reply, the signed-in person's task list, and a monthly sales chart. Everything on one screen, without going into four lists.

## Source of the data

A page has no data of its own: it shows what is in the system, prepared beforehand as a summary or a report. That is why the numbers on the page and in the list always agree, and a change to a record shows on the dashboard after a refresh.

You see only the data you have access to, and only in the company you are working in. After switching the company, the same page shows different numbers.

## Incorrect page contents

The scope and the way of counting are set by the administrator when building the page. A discrepancy between a number on the dashboard and your expectation is usually a difference of definition, for example counting closed matters or those assigned to a department. Report it together with an example, not with the number alone.
