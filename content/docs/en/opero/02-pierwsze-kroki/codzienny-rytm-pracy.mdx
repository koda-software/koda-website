## Starting the working day

The first step after signing in is to review the task list. It contains matters directed by name to the signed-in user by another employee or by a process.

The system informs about a record handover automatically: **every assignment in a process generates a notification**, without any extra action by the person handing it over.

Notifications should be reviewed after tasks. Tasks mean work to be done; notifications are informational, which is why the reverse order delays handling of the matters that are waiting.

> **Example.** Recommended order: the task list, then notifications, and finally the lists you run. The review takes a few minutes and gives a full picture of the planned work.

## Documenting agreements at the record

Information about a matter should be at the record, not in external channels: a calendar, a notebook, or a messenger.

- **Record agreements in a comment at the record.** Agreements passed by a private message remain unavailable to the other people running the matter.
- **Attach files as attachments to the record.** An attachment at the matter is available to anyone who has access to it.
- **Enter deadlines in the deadline field.** A date recorded at the record is visible to the team and makes it possible to filter the list.

> **The record is the source of information about a matter.** Agreements not documented at the record stay outside the team's flow of information. A record at the record makes it possible to reconstruct the course of a matter regardless of the participants' memory.

## Working during the day

| Recommended practice | Rationale |
| --- | --- |
| Saving changes as you go | limits data loss when work is interrupted |
| Filling in fields at the moment a record is entered | filling in late requires re-establishing the data |
| Verifying the company context before adding a document | changing a document's company assignment after saving is sometimes impossible |
| Handing over tasks assigned by mistake | a task left without a response holds up other people's work |

The last item has a direct impact on the course of processes. A task assigned by mistake stays on the list until it is handed over, and the **matter is waiting for action**, which is not visible to the person waiting.

## Closing the working day

Actions that close the day, especially before days off and absence:

1. **Marking completed tasks.** A task that is done but not marked stays indistinguishable to the team from one that is not done.
2. **A comment on matters in progress**, if they may be taken over by another person.
3. **A review of deadlines falling in the coming days.**

Before a longer absence, additionally agree with your supervisor on the person who will take over open tasks.

## Practices not recommended

- **Creating another record in reaction to an error.** A duplicate stays in the list and affects the summaries. The right reaction is to report the problem.
- **Leaving descriptive fields empty** with the intention of filling them in later.
- **Skipping the process.** If a process makes work harder, report it; process changes are made in the configuration, not by skipping it in practice.
