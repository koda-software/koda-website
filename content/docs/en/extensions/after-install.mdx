This page covers an extension from the moment it is installed: its health, its three tabs, what members get, and what stays read-only. To install one, see [Install an extension](/docs/en/extensions/install); to upgrade or remove one, see [Upgrade and uninstall](/docs/en/extensions/upgrade-and-uninstall).

## The list of installed extensions

**Extensions** opens on **Installed**. Each row shows what the extension adds, its version and its status; an arrow next to the version means a newer one is available.

![Installed extensions with their status; three are inactive](/docs/_assets/extensions/after-install/installed-list.webp)

**Needs attention** shows only the extensions that do not run, and **Updates** only those with a newer version. Open an extension to see its page.

## States

The status next to the extension's name is one of these. When it does not run, a banner at the top of its page says why, and the button in the header opens the fix.

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Running** | Everything it needs is in place. Its steps and filters are offered in the builders. | Nothing. |
| **Needs settings** | A required setting has no value. | Press **Complete settings** and fill it in. |
| **Inactive** | The connection is not enabled. This is normal right after installing a package with a connection. | Press **Connect**, then test and activate the connection. |
| **Not running** | A module it needs is not available to this organization, or the catalog no longer offers it. | Enable the module, or ask the installation's administrator. |
| **Disabled** | An administrator switched it off. Everything stays in place and stops. | Choose **Enabled** from the status menu. |
| **Stopped part-way** | An install or upgrade failed in the middle. | Press **Retry**. The command resumes where it stopped. |
| **Installing**, **Uninstalling** | A command is running. | Wait; the other buttons are off until it ends. |

While an extension does not run, its steps and triggers are missing from the rule builder. A saved rule that reaches one of its steps fails at that step with `extension_inactive`, and no event of its triggers starts a rule. Its schedules wait, and members cannot use their connections. Its template filters keep working until it is disabled.

## Overview

**Overview** shows the description and **What it adds**: everything the extension installed, grouped by what it is for. Each item says whether rules use it.

Select an item to see its details: what it does, where it is used and, for a rule step, the steps it runs.

![A rule step's details: what it does, where it is used and how it works](/docs/_assets/extensions/after-install/content-step.webp)

The side column shows **Update available** when there is a newer version, **What it can reach** and **About** the package. **Package details** unfolds its checksum.

## Configuration

**Configuration** holds the extension's settings, its connection and what it was allowed to reach. Changing them needs `org.extensions.update`.

![The Configuration tab with a settings section and the connection](/docs/_assets/extensions/after-install/configuration.webp)

| Section | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Settings, one section per group | Fill in the values and press **Save changes**. A field you never set uses its default. While a required setting has no value, the extension does not run. |
| **Connection** | Enter the credentials and save, press **Test connection**, then **Activate**. The extension's steps run only while the connection is active. |
| **What it can reach** | Read only: the hosts and data access it was granted when it was installed. |

Settings apply to the whole organization. The form is read-only while a command runs for the extension, and the connection is read-only while the extension is disabled.

## Activity

**Activity** appears when the extension has rules, schedules or a member connection.

### Schedules

Each schedule shows how often it runs and for what, how many subjects it has (the organization, each active company or each connected member), and how many are due, running, failing or waiting to reconnect.

![The Activity tab: a schedule waiting for the extension, and Recent runs](/docs/_assets/extensions/after-install/activity-schedules.webp)

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **On schedule** | Runs have succeeded and none is failing. |
| **First run pending** | Nothing has run yet. Each new subject runs within its first interval. |
| **Not started yet** | No subjects yet. They are added within a minute of the extension running. |
| **No member connected** | A schedule for each member, and no member has connected yet. |
| **Some runs failing** | A subject's last run failed. It is retried with growing waits, never longer than the interval. A rate limit is not a failure. |
| **Waiting for the extension** | The extension does not run. When it runs again, each waiting subject runs once; missed runs are not repeated. |

A subject that stays due for more than a few minutes while the extension runs means Opero's background work is behind.

### Connected members

For a package with a [member connection](/docs/en/extensions/reference/member-connections), **Connected members** lists each member who connected their own account: their company, their **Last successful sync**, their state and the **Last error**.

![The Connected members section of an extension that no member has connected to yet](/docs/_assets/extensions/after-install/activity-members.webp)

A member in **Waiting for the member to reconnect** has to connect again: the service refused their account, an upgrade asks for more, or the company they chose is no longer theirs. Members connect on their own account page, under **Connected apps**.

### Recent runs

**Recent runs** lists the latest runs of the extension's rules. Viewing them needs permission to read rules.

## What members can use

When the package offers block buttons, record commands or record links, **Overview** lists them under **What members can use**.

![Actions and links members can use, each with the permission it needs](/docs/_assets/extensions/after-install/member-actions.webp)

A member uses one only in a company it is offered to, only if they are in its audience, and only with the permission it needs on their company role. After installing, each is offered to every company and all their members; **Manage access** narrows that.

## What the organization does next

The package brings parts; the organization decides where members meet them.

| Task | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Give the package permissions to company roles | **Roles**. Installing grants them to no one, so until then no member can use the block buttons, commands and links. |
| Place the package's blocks on pages | The page editor, like your own blocks. |
| Add the package's pages to a menu | **Menus**. The extension places none in a menu itself. |
| Narrow which companies and members see a block button, a command or a link | **Manage access** under **What members can use**. |
| Narrow which companies and members see a page | The page's details under **What it adds**. |
| Build rules on its steps and triggers | **Rules**. Its steps and triggers are in the rule builder's lists. |

## Managed assets

What an extension installs is managed by it: its rules, filters, module, objects, dictionaries, queries, blocks, forms, lists and pages. They change only when the extension is upgraded.

A managed asset opened from **What it adds** says so at the top and offers only what stays yours.

![A rule installed by an extension, shown read-only](/docs/_assets/extensions/after-install/managed-rule.webp)

| Asset | Read-only | Still yours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rules and template filters | Everything. A managed rule runs only through its step, never by hand or from another rule. | Nothing. |
| The module and its objects | Fields, the module itself, deleting them. | Records, which companies see them, record access, your own forms and lists on them. |
| Dictionaries | Entries, deleting it. | Using it in your own fields. |
| Saved queries | The SQL, deleting it. | Running it, using it in SQL lists, reports and blocks. |
| Blocks, forms, lists and pages | Their content. | Who may use each form, the object's default forms, a page's exposure and audience, menus, where blocks are placed. |

Managed rules, scripts, the module, objects, dictionaries and queries are left out of their normal management lists. Pickers in rules, lists, reports, forms and blocks still offer them.

## Disable an extension

Open the status menu next to the name and choose **Disabled**, then confirm **Disable extension**. The dialog lists the active rules that use its steps or triggers: until you enable it again, its triggers start nothing and its steps fail.

Everything it installed stays in place. Its data, forms, lists and pages keep working, its blocks show a notice instead of their content, and members' connections are kept. Choose **Enabled** to switch it back on.
